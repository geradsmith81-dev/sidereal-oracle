The Sidereal Oracle

Sidereal charts on the real constellations, read through William Lilly’s Christian Astrology (1647).

Free, with no ads, no trackers and no account. Every reading comes from fixed rules taken from Lilly’s book, each with its page: no AI chatbot writes them. Your birth details are never stored on a server or shared.

Put it on your home screen

Add this page to your home screen once. After that, the astrolabe icon opens the Oracle like any other app.

iPhone or iPad
  1. Open this page in Safari. Chrome on iPhone also works, using its own Share button.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow pointing up). On newer iPhones it may be inside the … menu first.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If you see a switch called Open as Web App, leave it on.
  4. You can change the name if you like, then tap Add.
Android phone or tablet
  1. Open this page in Chrome. Samsung Internet and Edge also work.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Install. On some phones the menu says Install app.
  4. The icon appears on your home screen and in your app list.
Computer
  1. In Chrome or Edge, look for the small Install icon at the right-hand end of the address bar, or use the browser menu → Install / Apps.
  2. Or simply bookmark this page.
Good to know
  • An internet connection is needed: the charts are calculated online from NASA JPL data.
  • If nobody has used the app for a while, it sleeps. Tap Streamlit’s wake-up button and give it up to a minute.
  • Privacy: your profile and birth details stay only in the open app and are cleared when you close it. Save a friend card if you want to keep them. This page uses no cookies and no tracking.
